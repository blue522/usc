How Early Decision & Early Action Change Your Admissions Odds

In 2026–2027, USC received 79,331 applications, admitted 10,006 students (12.61%), and enrolled 3,766 freshmen (37.64% yield). When you apply Early (ED or EA), two big things happen: (1) in ED, athletes and legacies quietly take a big slice of the early seats, and (2) if you aren't accepted early, you usually aren't rejected—you get pushed ("deferred") into Regular Decision for a 2nd Bite at the Apple!
